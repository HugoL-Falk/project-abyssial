## P26-07 — Supplies Dwindle opt2: Expand randomOutcome

### Problem
Both outcomes of "Send foragers" gave `gold +1`. Only difference was an optional `dread +1`. No genuine tension.

### Design

**opt2 "Send foragers"** — cost unchanged (`fol −1`). Expand to 4 equal-weight outcomes:

| Branch | Weight | Effects | Flavour |
|--------|--------|---------|---------|
| Windfall | 1 | `gold +2` | *"They return with more than expected. No one asks where it came from."* |
| Expected | 1 | `gold +1` | *"They return with enough. No one asks what enough means."* |
| Unease | 1 | `gold +1, dread +1` | *"They return with enough. One of them has stopped speaking."* |
| Seen | 1 | `insertCard selectman_has_questions, pos 3–6` | *"They return empty-handed. Someone followed them back with questions."* |

The "Seen" branch inserts `selectman_has_questions`, solving P26-02 reachability for that card.

## P26-13 — An Unremarkable Stump → Threat

### Problem
Currently a single-option treat (`dread −1`, return empty-handed). Too passive; the card is a dud outcome from `woodcutters_report`. The forest is cursed — finding a stump that shouldn't exist should cost something.

### Design

Tier: `treat` → `threat`. Two options, both net negative — different cost shapes.

**Title:** An Unremarkable Stump *(kept — irony lands harder now)*

**Body** (79 chars ✓):
> *"The roots go further down than the tree was tall. The silence is the wrong kind."*

**opt0 "Leave"** — `dread +2` (78 chars ✓):
> *"You leave. The image stays. It will be there when you close your eyes tonight."*

**opt1 "Wait at the treeline"** — `fol −1, dread +1` (69 chars ✓):
> *"You send them ahead. You wait at the treeline. They do not come back."*

### Notes
- opt1 follower loss reads as the companion being lost — no follow-up card needed; flavour carries the beat.
- No upstream card changes required; `woodcutters_report` "Buy the map" still inserts this card.
- Tier change from treat → threat is the only structural change outside this card's definition.

## P26-18 — The Opium Den / The Dreamer Restructure

### Problem
`the_dreamer` opt0 used `surfaceChainCard` to pull the god path card forward. User wants to remove this. Thematic restructure: the *dreamer/vision* framing belongs on Opium Den opt0, the *tangible payoff* on The Dreamer opt0.

### Design

#### The Opium Den (core.ts)

| | Label | Old effects | New effects |
|--|-------|-------------|-------------|
| opt0 | Encourage the visits | `fol −2, dread +2, setPrepTag opium_pact` | `fol −2, dread +2, insertCard the_dreamer` |
| opt1 | Forbid it | `inf −1, dread −1` | `fol −1, dread −1` |
| opt2 | Acquire it | `gold −2, inf +2, dread +1, insertCard the_dreamer` | `setPrepTag opium_pact, gold −2, dread +1` |

**opt0 condition:** `fol ≥ 2` (repeatable — `notHasPrepTag opium_pact` gate removed)
**opt1 condition:** none
**opt2 condition:** `gold ≥ 2 AND notHasPrepTag opium_pact` (one-time acquisition)

**Flavour texts** (all existing, verified still fit):
- opt0: *"They come back changed. The change is, for now, useful."* ✓
- opt1: *"The word goes out. Some comply. The rest just go on Wednesdays instead."* ✓ (fol loss fits better than inf loss narratively)
- opt2: *"The premises are modest. The access to its clientele is not."* ✓ ("access" = the opium_pact covenant)

#### The Dreamer (treats.ts)

| | Label | Old effects | New effects |
|--|-------|-------------|-------------|
| opt0 | Draw on the knowledge | `dread +1, surfaceChainCard pos 0, removeCard` | `relic +1, dread +1, removeCard` |
| opt1 | Set it aside | `gold +1, fol +1, inf +1, removeCard` | unchanged |

**opt0 flavour** updated (54 chars ✓):
> *"The translation is incomplete. It is enough to find it."*

opt1 flavour unchanged: *"The language resists translation. You leave the notes on the desk. They're still there in the morning."*

### Chain balance

| Path | Net resources |
|------|---------------|
| opt0 → Dreamer opt0 | `fol −2, dread +3, relic +1` |
| opt0 → Dreamer opt1 | `fol −1, dread +2, gold +1, inf +1` |
| opt1 | `fol −1, dread −1` |
| opt2 (one-time) | `gold −2, dread +1, opium_pact tag` |

opt0→Dreamer opt0 is the costly "go deep" line; steep but defensible given relic scarcity. opt0 is self-limiting (costs fol−2 + dread+2 per reshuffle).
